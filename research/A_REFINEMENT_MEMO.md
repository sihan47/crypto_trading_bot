# Final IS refinement: frozen three-arm comparison

The four-part specification was published in the conversation before implementation.

1. **Hypothesis:** relative positioning may explain bull-state continuation; funding may scale risk rather than gate entry. Prior ablations support investigating this but do not establish causality or prove that the seven-day exit caused profit concentration.
2. **Features/state:** all arms enter on bull regime and positive standardized positioning divergence, sharing valid-data eligibility. Control uses constant 0.5% risk and the original exits. Sizing-only uses 0.5% risk when the weighted funding feature is negative, otherwise 0.25%, decided once at entry. This is the user's discrete example, not a continuously differentiable sizing law or ongoing rebalancing. Full refinement uses the same entry/sizing and revised exits. Negative standardized funding does not necessarily mean a negative actual funding payment.
3. **Execution/risk:** cash spot, same next-15-minute-open execution, liquidity cap, fees 10bp and slippage 5bp each side. Full refinement replaces initial and trailing stops with three times 14-complete-day arithmetic true-range ATR, ratcheting from the highest completed 15-minute high since entry. Daily ATR is available only after all 24 hourly bars close; gaps invalidate the window. Trail changes apply only to later bars. Exit next open on macro bull-state failure or divergence z<=-1. No time stop in full refinement. Positions continue across 90-day reporting boundaries, with final sample liquidation only; separately run the legacy forced-flat window convention. Controls use the same daily ATR availability mask even though their risk rules do not use ATR, to avoid differing missing-data eligibility.
4. **Falsification:** report all arms including failures, top-three share of gross positive profit, top-three and top-5% removal PnL, 7/28-day circular-block bootstrap Sharpe intervals (2,000 draws), double-cost survival, and fixed +/-20% individual/joint perturbations of the new sizing, ATR length/multiple, and negative-divergence threshold. Do not pick the best variant or open the reserved 30%. These are exposed-history IS diagnostics, not selection-adjusted confidence or validated profitability.

The full arm bundles initial-stop, trailing-stop, time-exit and regime-exit changes. Three-arm results cannot causally attribute a difference to removal of the time stop alone. No production or VPS changes are made.
