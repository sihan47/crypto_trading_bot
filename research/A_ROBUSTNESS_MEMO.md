# A follow-up: frozen diagnostics, not a strategy search

Four-part specification was published in the conversation before implementation.

1. **Structural hypothesis:** low relative funding and relative positioning may add information to bull-state continuation. Forced short covering is not observed directly. `z(funding) * positive_OI_weight < 0` is algebraically just `z(funding)<0` wherever the OI inputs are valid. OI still supplies a missing-data eligibility mask, but no directional information. A positive divergence z-score means above its own historical mean, not necessarily a positive raw top/global ratio difference.
2. **Feature/regime diagnostics:** individually perturb the 30-day normalization, 90-day regime and 24-hour risk windows by +/-20%, plus both joint corners. Delay derivatives input records 1/2 hours, or delay the complete eligible entry event 1/2 hours while using current known risk estimates. Compare bull-only, bull+funding and bull+divergence on a common valid-data mask. No profitable regime or threshold is selected afterward.
3. **Execution/risk:** unchanged discovery-only spot engine, 0.5% risk budget, 10 bp fee plus 5 bp slippage per side, no leverage or funding payments, two-sigma initial/trailing stops and seven-day maximum hold. Same 365-day initialization and 90-day internal walk-forward windows. Final reserved 30% not loaded. Delayed events are stress scenarios, not newly recommended execution rules.
4. **Falsification:** negative window/delay outcomes undermine robustness; weak performance versus simpler components undermines the interaction interpretation. Report concentration, remove-best-5% realized PnL and per-fold contributions. Use 2,000 seeded circular seven-day daily-return block-bootstrap samples for descriptive uncertainty, not a corrected discovery test or posterior probability of profitability. Preserve original qualification criteria and do not promote a selected perturbation as the new baseline.

The equivalence audit initially detected missing-data differences in the diagnostic reconstruction. The OI history availability mask was restored before running the final results; baseline signals/stops are asserted equal to the established tournament. Delayed entry events retain source-time eligibility and also require current-time valid risk data. Tests verify prefix causality and this masking behavior.
